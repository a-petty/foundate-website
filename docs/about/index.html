Let’s talk

About

We are built to leave.

Foundate is an AI implementation practice. We build systems a business can trust, prove them against its own history, and hand them to the people who run them.

Why we exist

The incentive, inverted.

Most transformation partners are built to become indispensable. They embed, they bill indefinitely, and the capability walks out the door when they do.

We are built the other way round. We install something your people own and run, prove it against your own results, and then step back. The day you no longer need us is not the end of the value. It is the proof the value was real.

What we believe

Three beliefs behind every build.

Your independence is the product.

A system that depends on its builder is fragile. We hold our own work to that standard, including our own place in it. What we build is entirely yours.

Foundation before intelligence.

Trusted, well-defined data comes first. Insight built on numbers people quietly distrust is not insight; it is speculation.

Numbers are calculated, not generated.

Calculations stay deterministic and auditable. AI surfaces, explains and recommends. People decide, including whether and when any of it is automated.

How we are built

Small and senior, with the leverage of a large team.

AI makes extraordinary productivity possible, but our team still owns the results. Senior people set the architecture, own the checks and answer for every number. That is how a small practice delivers at a pace a conventional program staffs heavily to match.

We put the time it saves into the parts that protect you: proof, documentation and handover.

Who we are

Led by two Managing Partners.

Alec Petty

Managing Partner

Alec spent nearly a decade in finance & strategy before building AI systems full time: strategic finance at Peet’s Coffee, corporate strategy at Williams-Sonoma, and wealth management at Morgan Stanley and UBS. At Peet’s he led the rollout of a new enterprise planning system; at Williams-Sonoma he built the pricing, forecasting and process models leadership used to run Pottery Barn Brands. He has spent most of his career on the side of the table that signs off on the numbers. He learned early that a number nobody can trace is a number nobody should use, and he holds AI to the same standard. That is where Foundate’s insistence on proof comes from.

He holds a B.S. in Management Science from UC San Diego.

Byron Smith

Managing Partner

Byron spent a decade in investment banking and M&A advisory: most recently as a Vice President at Morgan Stanley covering healthcare technology, and before that at Wells Fargo and SunTrust Robinson Humphrey (now Truist). His work spanned buy-side and sell-side M&A, IPOs and strategic positioning. He later advised founders building and selling AI and technology companies, which showed him the gap between what AI can do and how businesses actually adopt it. That gap led him to co-found Foundate. His background shapes how he approaches AI: as a question of where it changes the economics of a business, not which tools are new.

He holds a Bachelor’s in Business Administration from USC’s Marshall School of Business and is completing his MBA at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, focusing on AI and data analytics.

The name

Foundate /ˈfaʊn.deɪt/ · verb

An emerging verb meaning “to be the foundation of,” or to act as the base for something else. It is what we ask of every system we build: the rest of the business should be able to stand on it.
