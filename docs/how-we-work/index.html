Let’s talk

How we work

Every system ships with its own proof.

This is the part most AI projects skip, and it is the reason the people accountable for the results do not trust them.

What we learned

AI projects rarely fail on the model. They stall on definitions, trust and access.

01

The math is the easy part.

In every build, the math was never what held things up. The real work was agreeing what counts as an active account, a renewal or a cancellation, and writing it down where everyone can see it.

02

If nobody can check it, nobody uses it.

The person accountable for a result has to be able to trace it to its source and its rule. Otherwise the old spreadsheet stays in charge.

03

Access and IT set the timeline.

Credentials, hosting and approvals cause more delay than any model. We plan the path to production on day one and build where your company already works.

The standard

Six things that have to be true before we call it done.

Not a process chart. Six conditions, each checkable, that a system meets before anyone relies on it.

  • It reproduces results you already trust.

    Your last signed-off period is the answer key. Every difference is accounted for. Zero unexplained is the bar.

    A prior year’s budget and a published report, rebuilt cell for cell.

  • Every number is calculated, never generated.

    One deterministic engine runs the checks and the live system. AI explains; it does not invent a figure.

  • Bad inputs stop the run.

    Missing or stale data blocks the result. The checks are never loosened to get a pass.

    A payout run that halted on two missing inputs.

  • Someone who did not build it tries to break it.

    A separate reviewer recomputes every output and attacks each conclusion before go-live.

    On one build, dozens of issues found and fixed before anyone saw a figure.

  • Its limits are written down.

    What the checks cannot see is stated at handover. “We cannot explain this yet” is an acceptable answer. A guess is not.

  • A named owner has run it without us.

    Done means your team has made a change and fixed a problem themselves. If it still needs us, it is not done.

If a result cannot be checked, we say so and do not build it.

What you keep

We build it, prove it and hand it over. You keep it. You don’t keep us.

Most firms are built to become indispensable. We are built to leave. The work is finished only when your team runs it without us.

What you own

  • The working system, on your own platforms
  • The rules, written down where your team can read them
  • The checks, the runbook and the approval trail

What changes for your people

Today, skilled people spend their weeks as the glue between systems: pulling data, re-keying it, reconciling it. After the handover, the same people oversee the automation that does that work, review what it produces, and spend their time on judgment and decisions.

Built where your company already works.

Your data warehouse, your workspace platform, your single sign-on. IT approves a familiar path instead of a new system, and the work does not stall waiting for infrastructure nobody owns.

Engagements

One workflow proves the process.

We start small on purpose. One process, proven on your own history, shows your team how the work is done before anyone commits to more.

  1. Step 1

    Working session

    Bring the process that takes too long or cannot be trusted. We map it with you and tell you plainly whether it is worth automating.

  2. Step 2

    Build and prove

    We build the system and prove it against your own history, with every difference explained, before anyone relies on it.

  3. Step 3

    Hand over, then decide what comes next

    Your team takes the system, the runbook and the approvals. Then we decide together what comes next. Each process gets its own proof; nothing is copied across.
