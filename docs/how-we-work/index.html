Let’s talk

How we work

We rebuild the foundation of a business, one process at a time.

Every company runs on processes that grew up before AI: a report assembled by hand, a queue nobody can see into, a number that lives in one person’s spreadsheet. We restructure them on the platforms you already run, so automation can take them over and anyone can check the result.

Two foundations

A business stands on two foundations. We rebuild the second, on top of the first.

01

The platform your business runs on.

Your ERP, your data warehouse, your workspace platform: the systems of record that hold what the business knows. We do not replace them. Everything we build runs on them, where your data already lives and IT already has control.

02

The processes that run on it.

Each function has its own: how sales qualifies a lead, how finance closes the month and plans the year, how operations reports what happened. Each is a foundation in its own right, with its own definitions, inputs and checks, and each can be restructured and automated on its own. That is the work.

03

Why that order.

Automate a process before its definitions and checks are settled and you get the old confusion, faster. Settle them first, and the automation that follows is something the people accountable for it can read, check and own.

What you keep

We build it, prove it and hand it over. You keep it. You don’t keep us.

Most firms are built to become indispensable. We are built to leave. The work is finished only when your team runs it without us.

What you own

  • The working system, on your own platforms
  • The rules, written down where your team can read them
  • The checks, the runbook and the approval trail

What changes for your people

Today, skilled people spend their weeks as the glue between systems: pulling data, re-keying it, reconciling it. After the handover, the same people oversee the automation that does that work, review what it produces, and spend their time on judgment and decisions.

Built where your company already works.

A subscription product is built for everyone and bolted onto your business. What we build is designed for the platform your business already runs on, and it stays yours.

01

Your platform, not another one.

Your data warehouse, your workspace platform, your single sign-on. IT approves a familiar path instead of a new system, and nothing stalls waiting for infrastructure nobody owns.

02

The model follows your stack.

Each agent runs on the model that fits the systems it works inside, open-source where that is the better fit. You are never tied to one vendor’s prices or roadmap.

03

Cost after handover is a design constraint.

Usage-based AI pricing moves in one direction. We size every system so that what it costs to run a year after we leave is a number you would sign off on.

Engagements
