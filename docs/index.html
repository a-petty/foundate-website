What we built

Built, proven and in use.

Two systems from one company-wide engagement across six teams. The rest are on the work page.

Legal

One docket for every legal request.

Requests arrived through a ticket queue, chat and email, and counsel had no single view of the workload. We built one tracked docket with aging, stale-ticket review, an activity trail and reporting on volume and time to close. It runs in production behind the company’s single sign-on.

ProvedWalked through with the legal team, who asked for changes. Those changes were built and merged within about a week.

Operations and Finance

Quarterly bonuses, calculated and checked before payroll.

A hand-maintained, multi-tab spreadsheet became one calculation engine. Exceptions are explicit, missing inputs block payroll instead of being guessed, and a second person approves before any payout file exists. It now runs the quarterly payout.

ProvedA separate check found payout errors in the old spreadsheet that the manual process had missed. The new calculation reproduced a closed quarter exactly.

How we work

Every system ships with its own proof.

Six conditions, met before anyone relies on a number.

  • Reproduces results you already trust.

    Your signed-off history is the answer key. Zero unexplained differences is the bar.

  • Calculated, never generated.

    One deterministic engine for the checks and the live system. AI explains; it does not invent.

  • Bad inputs stop the run.

    Missing or stale data blocks the result. The checks are never loosened to get a pass.

  • Someone else tries to break it.

    A reviewer who did not build it recomputes every output before go-live.

  • Limits written down.

    What the checks cannot see is stated at handover, not discovered later.

  • Owned by your team.

    Done means your people have run it, changed it and fixed it without us.
